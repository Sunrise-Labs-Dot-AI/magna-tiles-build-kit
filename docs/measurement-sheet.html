Magna-Tiles Measurement Sheet

Print at 100% / actual size. Do NOT "Fit to page" or "Scale to fit". After printing, lay a ruler across the labeled 100 mm bar near the top of the sheet — it must measure exactly 100 mm. If it doesn't, reprint at 100%.

  1. Lay one tile flat in the grid area, near the center.
  2. Photograph directly overhead — phone parallel to the page. All four corner markers (A, B, C, D) must be visible in the frame.
  3. One tile per photo. Send each with the shape name in the message (e.g. "right triangle", "small square").
  4. Bonus shots: a close-up of a tile edge showing magnet positions, and two tiles snapped together along a shared edge viewed edge-on.

The four corner markers are at known coordinates: A=(10, 65) mm, B=(205, 65) mm, C=(10, 255) mm, D=(205, 255) mm. Their pairwise distances (AB = 195 mm, AC = 190 mm, AD ≈ 272.3 mm) give a per-axis pixel scale in the photo and correct any perspective tilt.

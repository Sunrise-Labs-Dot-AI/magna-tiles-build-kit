# MAGNA-TILES Engineering Reference for 3D Reconstruction

Prepared for software reconstruction of physical MAGNA-TILES and edge-compatible magnetic-tile builds. This reference prioritizes verified geometry and mechanics over completeness: dimensions that are not published by MAGNA-TILES are marked as unpublished rather than inferred as production specifications.

## 1. PIECE CATALOG & GEOMETRY

## 2. MAGNET MECHANICS & CONNECTION RULES

A square MAGNA-TILES piece has two magnets on a side according to Rothman-Shore’s teardown-style analysis, and the Fiddler analysis models eight magnets around the perimeter of a square tile ([Rothman-Shore magnet analysis](https://rothmanshore.com/2013/03/03/the-secret-of-how-magna-tiles-work/), [Fiddler Magna-Tiles analysis](https://thefiddler.substack.com/p/can-you-figure-out-how-magna-tiles)). The Fiddler analysis places those modeled square magnets at one-quarter and three-quarters of the way along each edge, but that placement is an analytical model rather than an official MAGNA-TILES drawing ([Fiddler Magna-Tiles analysis](https://thefiddler.substack.com/p/can-you-figure-out-how-magna-tiles)).

MAGNA-TILES says it uses high-quality ceramic, ferrite magnets, and the patent describes plastic magnets with ferrite particles in a polymeric binder in one disclosed tile construction ([MAGNA-TILES FAQ](https://magnatiles.com/pages/faq), [US9636600B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US9636600B2/en)). The magnet poles are oriented perpendicular to the tile face in the patent and in Rothman-Shore’s explanation, which means the relevant north-south axis is out of the tile plane rather than along the edge direction ([US9636600B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US9636600B2/en), [Rothman-Shore magnet analysis](https://rothmanshore.com/2013/03/03/the-secret-of-how-magna-tiles-work/)).

The magnets should be modeled as fixed-orientation magnets, not as freely rotating magnets, because the patent contrasts the disclosed fixed-polarity approach with prior-art rotating magnets and states that alternating polarity along mating edges is preferred for connection flexibility when tiles are flipped or rotated ([US9636600B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US9636600B2/en)). Rothman-Shore reports that each edge’s two magnets are oriented in opposite directions and that the alternating pattern makes tiles attract regardless of rotation or flip ([Rothman-Shore magnet analysis](https://rothmanshore.com/2013/03/03/the-secret-of-how-magna-tiles-work/)).

| Rule | Engineering interpretation for reconstruction | Source and caveat |
|---|---|---|
| Full equal-length edge alignment | This is the highest-confidence snap: all edge magnets intended for that shared edge overlap in a predictable polarity pattern. | Rothman-Shore explains the alternating two-magnet edge pattern, and the patent describes common-edge interfaces across multiple angles ([Rothman-Shore magnet analysis](https://rothmanshore.com/2013/03/03/the-secret-of-how-magna-tiles-work/), [US9636600B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US9636600B2/en)). |
| Different edge lengths | A shorter magnetic edge can attach along part of a longer magnetic edge if the magnet spacing is compatible. | The patent explicitly gives an example where a longer edge contains two magnet pairs and two shorter square edges can attach along it ([US9636600B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US9636600B2/en)). |
| Half-tile lateral offset | A half-edge offset between otherwise matching square edges can repel rather than attract. | Rothman-Shore states that the repulsion case is a misaligned placement where the top half of one tile touches the bottom half of another ([Rothman-Shore magnet analysis](https://rothmanshore.com/2013/03/03/the-secret-of-how-magna-tiles-work/)). |
| Partial overlap | Partial overlap can be physically possible, but the connection is weaker or unstable if fewer magnet pairs overlap. | The weaker-partial-overlap conclusion is an engineering inference from the patent’s discussion of magnet spacing and attraction along mating edges, not a MAGNA-TILES-published force table ([US9636600B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US9636600B2/en)). |
| Dihedral hinge angle | Model an edge connection as a magnetic hinge over a broad angle range rather than as a fixed 90° joint. | The patent discusses connected tiles at 0°, 90°, 180°, acute, and obtuse angles and states that magnetic attraction does not vary by more than a factor of two over 45° to 180° along a common edge interface ([US9636600B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US9636600B2/en)). |
| Detents | No mechanical detent angle was found in the sources reviewed; 90° is important for cubes but is not described as a locking detent. | The patent says the 90° orientation is important for hollow cubes and rectangular hexahedrons, but it describes pivoting under magnetic force rather than a detent mechanism ([US9636600B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US9636600B2/en)). |

Practical impossible or invalid connections should be separated into geometric impossibility and magnetic invalidity. A single rigid tile cannot connect one of its own edges to another of its own edges without bending, and no source reviewed here describes flexible Classic MAGNA-TILES pieces ([MAGNA-TILES Classic 100 product page](https://www.magnatiles.com/product/magna-tiles-classic-100-piece-set/), [US9636600B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US9636600B2/en)). A full-edge snap between the right triangle’s about 5.196 in long leg and a 3 in or 6 in edge should be rejected as a full-edge geometric match because the long-leg length is mathematically distinct from both the short and long edge classes ([NKY MakerSpace connection guide](http://www.nkymakerspace.com/uploads/5/6/7/9/56796803/magnatilesconnectionguidethursday.pdf)). A half-tile lateral offset should be treated as a magnetic repulsion case rather than a valid attractive snap because Rothman-Shore identifies that offset as the way to make two MAGNA-TILES repel ([Rothman-Shore magnet analysis](https://rothmanshore.com/2013/03/03/the-secret-of-how-magna-tiles-work/)).

### Confidence & Gaps

- Well sourced: square tiles have two magnets per side, square tiles have eight perimeter magnets in the Fiddler model, magnet polarity is out-of-plane, polarity alternates along edges, and MAGNA-TILES uses ferrite magnets ([Rothman-Shore magnet analysis](https://rothmanshore.com/2013/03/03/the-secret-of-how-magna-tiles-work/), [Fiddler Magna-Tiles analysis](https://thefiddler.substack.com/p/can-you-figure-out-how-magna-tiles), [MAGNA-TILES FAQ](https://magnatiles.com/pages/faq)).
- Uncertain: exact magnet positions, magnet dimensions, and magnet counts for triangles and Classic large squares are not published by MAGNA-TILES in the sources reviewed, so software should make those parameters configurable ([MAGNA-TILES FAQ](https://magnatiles.com/pages/faq), [MAGNA-TILES Classic 100 product page](https://www.magnatiles.com/product/magna-tiles-classic-100-piece-set/), [US9636600B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US9636600B2/en)).
- Unavailable: no official production pull force in newtons, no official detent specification, and no official CAD-level magnet layout by shape was found in the manufacturer pages, patent text, or maker analyses reviewed here ([MAGNA-TILES FAQ](https://magnatiles.com/pages/faq), [MAGNA-TILES quality article](https://magnatiles.com/blogs/news/why-are-magna-tiles-so-expensive), [US9636600B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US9636600B2/en)).

## 4. STRUCTURAL STABILITY

MAGNA-TILES structures are edge-connected magnetic assemblies whose joints can pivot over a broad angle range, so stability comes from base footprint, closed loops, bracing, and multi-tile junctions rather than from rigid locked hinges ([US9636600B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US9636600B2/en)). The patent states that magnetic tile systems provide higher magnetic attraction forces when more than two tiles are attached along a common edge interface, and it reports that a tile at 90° to two other tiles joined at 0° measured about 90 percent more pull strength than a tile attached at 90° to just one other tile ([US9636600B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US9636600B2/en)).

A documented record tower reached 50 ft 8 in using almost 4,000 MAGNA-TILES, and the MAGNA-TILES article says the build started with a sturdy base and fell after a slight wobble followed by a gentle sway ([MAGNA-TILES Guinness tower article](https://magnatiles.com/blogs/news/a-tower-of-hope-dashel-prywes-sets-world-record-with-magna-tiles%C2%AE-to-raise-awareness-for-childhood-kidney-disease-ckd)). This tower demonstrates that very tall structures are possible with enough pieces and process control, but the article does not publish the base footprint, center-of-mass data, or per-joint force measurements ([MAGNA-TILES Guinness tower article](https://magnatiles.com/blogs/news/a-tower-of-hope-dashel-prywes-sets-world-record-with-magna-tiles%C2%AE-to-raise-awareness-for-childhood-kidney-disease-ckd)).

Engineering Emily’s tower-building article states that a strong base enables a tall and stable tower, that the wider and stronger the base is, the taller the tower can be, and that a triangle tower falls more easily than a pentagon tower because the triangle tower has a smaller base ([Engineering Emily tower article](https://www.engineeringemily.com/easy-fun-tower-building-activity-teach-toddlers-shapes-gravity/)). The same article states that a tower eventually falls when the base can no longer support the blocks at the top, which is consistent with modeling failure as a center-of-gravity and base-footprint problem ([Engineering Emily tower article](https://www.engineeringemily.com/easy-fun-tower-building-activity-teach-toddlers-shapes-gravity/)).

MAGNA-TILES says its products undergo drop testing, torque testing, tension testing, compression testing, tension tests for magnets, impact tests for magnets, and soaking tests for magnets, but the article does not publish pass/fail thresholds or numerical pull forces ([MAGNA-TILES quality article](https://magnatiles.com/blogs/news/why-are-magna-tiles-so-expensive)). The same article says the reinforced lattice maximizes stiffness and strength of the tile surface area, press pins and rivets help keep tiles secured, and the V-Lock Connection design helps prevent chipping and edge separation ([MAGNA-TILES quality article](https://magnatiles.com/blogs/news/why-are-magna-tiles-so-expensive)).

| Build situation | Rule of thumb for reconstruction or validation | Evidence level |
|---|---|---|
| Tall tower | Require a closed polygonal base and penalize height-to-footprint ratios that place the center of mass outside the base. | The base-width principle is directly sourced, while center-of-mass validation is engineering inference from the same stability description ([Engineering Emily tower article](https://www.engineeringemily.com/easy-fun-tower-building-activity-teach-toddlers-shapes-gravity/)). |
| Open wall | Treat a wall made only of square panels as shear-prone because edge joints act like hinges. | The hinge behavior is sourced from the patent’s broad angle range, while shear-collapse behavior is engineering inference ([US9636600B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US9636600B2/en)). |
| Closed box or cube | Treat closed faces and multi-tile junctions as much stronger than isolated edge pairs. | The patent’s multi-tile junction and 90 percent pull-strength improvement support this rule ([US9636600B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US9636600B2/en)). |
| Ramp | Brace the slope with side triangles or rails and avoid unsupported cantilevered rolling surfaces. | Official ramp videos use triangles or rails for ramp geometry, but no load rating is published ([Build Car Ramps with Henry the MAGNA-TILES Engineer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vxwBYubszZ8), [Build a Car Ramp with MAGNA-TILES](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tDTWoHOAPeY)). |

## 5. CONSTRUCTION TECHNIQUES

A flat net can be folded into a 3D solid because the NKY MakerSpace guide states that nets are 2D flat patterns folded to form 3D shapes, and the patent describes folding an extended L-shaped tile along lines to bring magnets into proximity and form an open half-cube structure ([NKY MakerSpace connection guide](http://www.nkymakerspace.com/uploads/5/6/7/9/56796803/magnatilesconnectionguidethursday.pdf), [US9636600B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US9636600B2/en)). For Classic pieces, software can model “build flat, then fold” as a sequence of coplanar edge joins followed by rotations around shared magnetic edges, because the patent describes connected tiles at multiple angles and the NKY guide emphasizes visualizing how a net folds ([US9636600B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US9636600B2/en), [NKY MakerSpace connection guide](http://www.nkymakerspace.com/uploads/5/6/7/9/56796803/magnatilesconnectionguidethursday.pdf)).

| Technique | Short how-to | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Cube or box | Use six square faces for a hollow cube, or use a cross-like net and fold four wall faces upward before adding the top face. | Go Science Kids states that six square sides make a cube, and the NKY guide states that nets fold into 3D shapes ([Go Science Kids geometry article](https://gosciencekids.com/index.html%3Fp=1574.html), [NKY MakerSpace connection guide](http://www.nkymakerspace.com/uploads/5/6/7/9/56796803/magnatilesconnectionguidethursday.pdf)). |
| Square substitution | Replace one small square with two right triangles where a build source permits a square made of right triangles. | MAGNA-ABBY says two right triangles make a square, and Go Science Kids states that two right angle triangles can make a small square ([3 Easy MAGNA-TILES Builds with MAGNA-ABBY](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aWdnNoTtFOY), [Go Science Kids geometry article](https://gosciencekids.com/index.html%3Fp=1574.html)). |
| Triangular prism or wedge | Use two triangle end faces and connect square or rectangular faces between corresponding edges. | Go Science Kids states that a triangular prism has two triangles on the ends and squares or rectangles on the other sides ([Go Science Kids geometry article](https://gosciencekids.com/index.html%3Fp=1574.html)). |
| Ramp | Use triangles to set the angle or turn and use larger panels or rails as the rolling surface when available. | The official Henry video uses isosceles triangles for the small ramp angle, equilateral triangles for a medium-ramp turn, and XL squares for a big ramp ([Build Car Ramps with Henry the MAGNA-TILES Engineer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vxwBYubszZ8)). |
| Braced slope | Add side triangles, rails, or closed side faces so the slope is not only a single hinged panel. | This is engineering inference from official ramp examples and the patent’s free-angle edge behavior ([Build Car Ramps with Henry the MAGNA-TILES Engineer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vxwBYubszZ8), [US9636600B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US9636600B2/en)). |
| Symmetry and mirroring | Build one side of a vehicle, animal, aircraft, or ramp support and mirror the edge sequence on the opposite side. | The official dog, butterfly, dinosaur, and jet references are visually symmetric or bilaterally structured, but symmetry as an algorithmic construction rule is an engineering inference from those references ([MAGNA-TILES 3 Fun Building Ideas](https://magnatiles.com/blogs/news/get-started-with-3-fun-magna-tiles%C2%AE-building-ideas), [MAGNA-TILES Dinosaur Builds PDF](https://www.magnatiles.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/2D-Dinosaur-Builds.pdf), [Magna-Tiles Jet Aircraft video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WDtC_9se3ds)). |
| Subassemblies | Build stable modules such as boxes, ramp supports, wings, or tower levels, then join those modules. | The patent states that stable larger assemblies may be easier to build by fabricating subassemblies and joining them rather than adding one tile at a time ([US9636600B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US9636600B2/en)). |

### Confidence & Gaps

- Well sourced: cube construction, right-triangle square substitution, triangular prisms, flat nets, and subassemblies are all documented by official or maker/education sources ([Go Science Kids geometry article](https://gosciencekids.com/index.html%3Fp=1574.html), [3 Easy MAGNA-TILES Builds with MAGNA-ABBY](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aWdnNoTtFOY), [NKY MakerSpace connection guide](http://www.nkymakerspace.com/uploads/5/6/7/9/56796803/magnatilesconnectionguidethursday.pdf), [US9636600B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US9636600B2/en)).
- Uncertain: bracing rules for specific loads and symmetry extraction from videos are engineering interpretations, not manufacturer-published algorithms ([Build Car Ramps with Henry the MAGNA-TILES Engineer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vxwBYubszZ8), [Magna-Tiles Jet Aircraft video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WDtC_9se3ds)).
- Unavailable: no official machine-readable build graph, vertex-coordinate list, or assembly-order file was found for the official build ideas reviewed here ([MAGNA-TILES 3 Fun Building Ideas](https://magnatiles.com/blogs/news/get-started-with-3-fun-magna-tiles%C2%AE-building-ideas), [MAGNA-TILES Spring Builds PDF](https://www.magnatiles.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Spring-Downloads.pdf), [MAGNA-TILES Dinosaur Builds PDF](https://www.magnatiles.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/2D-Dinosaur-Builds.pdf)).

**Required Findings**

1. **Cross-group magnetic contact can be certified as a “first placement.”**
   The proposal accepts a first placement when no fixed part exists in the moving part’s declared group, but it does not require proving the moving part is also magnetically separated from every other group. `componentContacts` only rejects declared graph connections crossing groups and raw solid overlap, not valid magnetic edge contact between groups. That means the new closure rule could pass a first panel that is already snapped to the unrelated released obstacle, bypassing the “separated approach” invariant.

2. **Workspace floor alignment can teleport released predecessor state.**
   The plan says not to teleport predecessor states, but workspace stages still derive `floorY` from the new nominal stage and then `workspaceBuild` shifts the predecessor build to that floor. The stored `EngineState` is reused, but the public build is vertically re-based to the new stage floor. Without pinning workspace floor to the predecessor’s actual floor, a “beside an occupied workspace” fixture can pass after silently moving the obstacle up or down. The acceptance list does not include a negative for mismatched new-module floor height.
   References: [assembly.ts](</Users/jamesheath/Documents/Claude/Projects/Worktrees/magnatiles-footage-replicas/lib/replication/assembly.ts:137>), [workspace.ts](</Users/jamesheath/Documents/Claude/Projects/Worktrees/magnatiles-footage-replicas/lib/replication/workspace.ts:25>), [runs/2026-09-27-independent-module-start-plan.md](</Users/jamesheath/Documents/Claude/Projects/Worktrees/magnatiles-footage-replicas/runs/2026-09-27-independent-module-start-plan.md:5>)

3. **The proposed tests do not prove predecessor dynamics were retained, only that a final checkpoint exists.**
   The acceptance asks to “retain released predecessor bodies” and “require all nine placed parts to survive the final free checkpoint,” but the result surface exposes checkpoint summaries, not terminal body identities or poses. A regression could reconstruct or rebase the predecessor and still report a nine-body free checkpoint. The required test needs to assert continuity of predecessor state across operations, for example unchanged predecessor body ids/poses before and after the support build, not just final `dynamicTileCount` or pass status.

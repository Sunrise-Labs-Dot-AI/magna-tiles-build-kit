Because it is partly edge-on and partly overlapped by orange transparent tiles, I would count only the clearly bounded yellow triangle, not background yellow/orange seen through it.

Its vertical edge is visible just behind the front-left roof/side area. At 80.5s, the blue triangular tile is then placed across the top/turn area. So the evidence supports: **upright blue side panel first, blue top/turn panel afterward.**

- **Initial upper green/orange side modules:** At 85.0s and 91.0s, the upper structure shows green/orange triangular side modules along the sloped upper section. Some green/orange triangular faces are solidly bounded, but several apparent colors are visible through transparent overlapping tiles. I would only treat the clearly edged green triangle on the upper side and the orange triangular face beside it as established; I would not infer a full tessellated wall or hidden triangle count from translucency alone.

**Bottom line:** The initial red/orange module can be called **two triangles with confidence**, but **not three** from these frames alone. And yes, the blue upright side panel appears before the blue top turn is added.

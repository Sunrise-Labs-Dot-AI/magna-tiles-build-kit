The isolated f64 runtime initially passed 198 contact cases while application hull coordinates were still quantized through `Float32Array`. It also passed the loaded-frequency acceptance pattern and 108 independent closed-shell cases. Two clean builds have identical CJS/MJS/WASM hashes. A canonical collider orientation with full precision performs worse (158/198 pass). Thus the earlier 198/198 result does not select f64: input rounding accidentally masked the defect. Reject precision-only as a remedy, retain all failed results, and do not promote a vendored runtime. Continue diagnosing the native GJK/manifold construction on the exact-coordinate failures.

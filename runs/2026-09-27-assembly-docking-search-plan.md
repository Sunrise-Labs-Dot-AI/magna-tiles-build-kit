# Bound assembly search across complete stage attempts

Full loop, internal draft branch only. The contact runtime remains separately under integration review. Do not change source parts, dimensions, material constants, simulation duration, rest criteria, or solid/magnetic/hand/path gates.

The reviewed clear-first docking order is useful for the launch seam but is not a complete construction planner. Fresh wedge construction with it passes the upper driving panel, then fails to settle while inserting the lower panel. The original mean-first proposal order passes that wedge, while the launch's final free release fails. A proposal cannot be selected solely from its own static checks when later operations also need its clearances.

Proposal: evaluate at most two deterministic docking policies per whole stage: `clear-first` (reviewed new order) then `support-aligned` (original bounded proposals, mean first). A policy is passed explicitly to all docking calls, including prepared transfers; no module-ID or source-specific branch. Each whole-stage attempt begins from the same actual predecessor workspaces and reruns all three perturbation seeds and all placements, supports, carries, closure and free checkpoints. Use one policy for the entire stage and all seeds. Do not combine successful seeds from different attempts. Retain failed attempt evidence and only append terminal workspaces from a complete passing attempt. Never mutate or substitute a failed branch into a successor. Respect the original deadline across attempts; no extra time silently granted. Both policies reject nonfinite and above-original-limit cross penetrations and report the selected target penetration. The support-aligned policy grants no exemption from any dynamic gate.

Use a small stage-evaluation helper extracted from the current per-stage implementation, without changing operation semantics. Top-level result has selected operations/checkpoints as today, selected policy, and a separate list of rejected complete attempts with their status/details/operations/checkpoints. If both fail, return failure with both records and prepare nothing. Invalid contracts/unverified coverage do not trigger a second attempt; budget exceptions remain exceptions.

Tests must prove isolated retry state and predecessor immutability; clear-first short-circuits on full success; a later failure retries the whole stage from its original inputs; no cross-attempt seed mixing; both failures prevent a dependent stage; thrown budget is not retried or converted to success; fallback docking never bypasses an injected carry/solid/release failure; selected and failed target penetration metrics survive result serialization. Fresh actual small construction is then rerun with all original physics gates, followed by code review. A failed trajectory remains failed evidence; search merely selects a different fully simulated trajectory.

## Review resolution

The stage helper returns private attempt operations/checkpoints/states/failures, and selected result/prepared arrays are touched only after full success. Clone the chosen predecessor's build/state/hands/components for each seed, separately for each attempt. Discard every terminal workspace from a failed attempt, even successful seeds; rejected reporting excludes reusable states. No catch converts `SimulationBudgetExceeded`, and check the unchanged deadline before every attempt. Results expose `dockingPolicy` (null on failure), `attemptedPolicies`, and `rejectedAttempts` with per-attempt evidence. Both failed policies leave selected operations/checkpoints empty. Add required-policy transfer and predecessor mutation tests.

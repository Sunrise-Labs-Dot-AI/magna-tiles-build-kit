# Check numerical contact under independently defined heavier loads

Next bounded increment after supported transfer is published. Do not modify the currently frozen validator or interrupt its final validation.

## Evidence motivating the work

The prior closed-source-support hypotheses failed the fixed 0.03-inch table guard, while smaller isolated fixtures passed. A new independent fixture repeats the existing inverted catalog shell vertically, with a single horizontal floor and four upright squares per level. It has no source-derived geometry, wedge, source-fitted angles or altered mass. Five, nine, thirteen and seventeen panels pass release on seeds 0/17/53. All three 25-panel fixtures fail at 0.0300065–0.0300324 inches of peak table penetration. Existing flat/five-panel contact sweeps still pass and select 120 Hz. These results expose a load-coverage gap; they do not prove the source topology or establish physical magnet/material values.

## Bounded experiment and acceptance

Promote the independent loaded-shell generator into the contact fixture family only after checking its catalog solids, exact distinct panels and valid magnetic edges. Preserve all current flat, five-panel load and unsupported-hinge fixtures. Record unchanged baseline outcomes and every failed numerical trial.

Run a predefined numerical convergence matrix at contact frequencies 120/240/480 Hz, integration rates 960/1920 Hz and solver counts 16/32, with seeds 0/17/53. Include the flat, five-panel, nine-panel, thirteen-panel, seventeen-panel and twenty-five-panel fixtures. Retain the whole observed load family because articulated contact behavior need not be monotonic. Keep all masses, gravity, friction, magnet parameters, tile dimensions, drop, perturbations, durations, overlap/penetration limits and rest criteria fixed. Inspect actual table/contact peaks at every integration step up to completion or the first failure, not just settled depth. Use an explicitly fail-fast diagnostic runner: preserve the first failure, actual simulated duration and terminal snapshot, then stop that cell. Failed cells have unavailable late/rest metrics when the required interval was not reached; do not advance a frozen failed engine or report fictitious recovery. Qualifying cells must complete all 7.5 simulated seconds. Use the existing calibration requirements: peak penetration at most 0.03 inches, late penetration at most 0.01 inches, 7.5 seconds simulated, late interval from 6.75 seconds, displacement at most 0.95 inches and at least 90 reporting rest steps. Publication of any source release still requires its stricter existing release criteria. No trial that records a solid failure may pass even if it later recovers.

Only 120 and 240 Hz are eligible for runtime selection in this matrix. The 480 Hz rows are diagnostic only because the declared integration rates sample that period too sparsely; selecting 480 requires a separately reviewed finer-rate experiment including at least 3840 Hz. Choose the lowest eligible frequency only if every one of its cells passes the whole declared fixture/seed matrix and additionally has peak penetration at most 0.025 inches and late penetration at most 0.008 inches. These stricter selection margins leave room below the unchanged runtime guards; a near-boundary result remains diagnostic. For each fixed fixture × seed × frequency, compute spread across all four rate × solver cells, and require all four to pass before applying the sensitivity test. Require peak-penetration spread at most 0.005 inches and late-penetration spread at most 0.002 inches. These sensitivity limits are declared before the matrix. Record settled poses and active/popped joints to expose articulated-shape changes; do not call contact convergence a material or magnet calibration. Treat material tuning and contact numerics separately. If none passes, retain the failure and investigate the actual collision/joint event before changing any other setting. Do not increase a limit, accept a near miss, add supports or infer hidden source parts.

A parameter change is permitted only after this independent evidence supports it. Then run unchanged assembly/grip/release/vehicle regressions, including the unsupported free hinge rejection and both one-hand and supported transfer. Check that no caller silently overwrites the selected parameter. Regenerate all fixtures and all four source reports under one final validator hash. Compare the complete source verdicts and actual physical outcomes against the previous checkpoint. Rerun the separate compact closed-support hypotheses; a change in their outcome remains a simulation diagnostic, never source-count or source-fidelity credit.
